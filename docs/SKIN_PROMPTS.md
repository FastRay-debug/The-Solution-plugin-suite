# Skin photos - what to generate

Every skin below has a slot in the plugins already. Make an image for it (any image generator, or your own photo), save it as
`UI/skins/<key>.jpg` (for the installer) or simply open it in the **Skin Editor** and save. Missing ones show a colour gradient until then.

**Style for every prompt (paste after the scene):** Ultra-detailed photorealistic image, 16:9.5 wide (1280x760 or larger, e.g. 2560x1520). Dark, moody, cinematic lighting, rich but not oversaturated colour. Keep the CENTRE calm and slightly darker (a 3D audio stage is drawn there) and leave the LEFT and RIGHT thirds uncluttered (knobs sit there). Top 60 px and bottom 60 px can be darker. No text, no logos, no badges, no emblems, no licence plates, no watermarks, no people's faces.

**Careful with brands:** car models and landmarks are fine to depict, but keep real logos, badges, school crests and store signs (for example a Kroger sign) out of
images you ship - those are trademarks. For Vidalia, your own photos (high school, Main Street, the onion fields) loaded into the Skin Editor are the best source.
